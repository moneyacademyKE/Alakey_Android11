## Rich Hickey pass
- **NetworkCall.kt (33)** — right idea (one retry helper, exponential backoff, `Result` as the channel) with one complection: it treats *cancellation* as just another `Exception`, so the abstraction leaks a lifecycle event into the data channel. `delay(currentDelay)` at :20 accidentally rescues the retry loop (it rethrows on a cancelled job), but the final attempt at :25-30 has no such accident. Simplest correct shape is a 3-line `suspendRunCatching` wrapper, not more retry logic.
- **FeedRepository.kt (102)** — the honest file in this layer: `looksLikeFeed` (:94-100) is a real pure predicate with `@VisibleForTesting`, and :41 `if (items.isEmpty()) throw Exception("No episodes found in feed")` is a genuine guard that stops RssParser's empty-list-on-parse-failure (:29-31) from looking like a legitimately empty feed. Its problem is that **all 8 of its `throw Exception(...)` sites are untyped strings** (:30, :31, :41, :73, :78, :79, :85, :86 — 12 tree-wide, the other 4 at DownloadRepository.kt:42, :43, :59, :68), so callers cannot distinguish offline from malformed from empty, which is precisely why everything downstream collapses into one generic message and one `printStackTrace`. Data over abstraction: a sealed `FeedError` would let M67 report *which* feeds failed and why.
- **FeedSyncWorker.kt (33)** — 6 lines of `doWork` that promise error handling and provide none (M67). The unused `e` at :20 means even the retry path logs nothing.
- **NetworkLogic.kt (30)** — both catches are correct *because both functions are pure and non-suspend* (:14, :23); cancellation cannot reach them. Do not "fix" these.
